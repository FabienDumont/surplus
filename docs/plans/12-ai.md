# 12 — AI

## Goal

Put something in the other seats. `CountryControl.Ai` is only a flag today, so a country the human
does not play does nothing while its population grows past what its branches can feed. After this
plan an AI country keeps its own reproduction going and ends the wars it is winning.

The AI is deliberately small: two rules, deterministic, using only commands a player also has. It
does not plan, it does not declare war, and it does not legislate.

## Changes

### `surplus.Application/Ai/CountryAi.cs` — namespace `Surplus.Application.Ai`

```csharp
public static class CountryAi
{
  /// Lets one AI country do what it does at the turn of a month.
  public static void Act(World world, Country country);
}
```

`Act` applies the two rules below, in order. It must never throw for want of something to do: where a
rule cannot be satisfied it does as much as it can and moves on.

### `Session.Advance` and `Session.AdvanceUntil`

Both pass their days through one private method since plan 07; put the AI's turn there. After
`World.Elapse` for a day that is the first of a month, call `CountryAi.Act` for every country
that is not played and is still in the world. Take a snapshot of the country list first: an AI peace
can remove a country mid-loop.

### Since plans 09 and 10

This plan was written before revenues and circulation. Read its rules this way:

- **"The store" is the country's total** (`Country.Store`), and the shortfall is counted
  country-wide. Circulation moves goods between provinces by itself, so the AI need not.
- **"Daily use" of a basket good is the country's claims** (plan 09): baskets claimed, not heads.
- **Taking on hands draws on the reserve army**, the idle of a wage-earning class, before any other.
  Where a province has a reserve army, the AI employs from it first.
- **Carriage is a commodity like any other.** When a province reports `CarriageFellShort`, the AI
  takes on carters in that province's carting branch by Rule 1.

### Rule 1 — keep every flow in balance

For each commodity, in register order, compute for this country:

- **daily use** = the population's subsistence in that commodity (`Subsistence.For(Population)`)
  plus what its own branches consume of it in a day (sum of `ConsumedInOnePeriod()` over its
  branches);
- **daily output** = sum over its branches producing it of `OutputPerHour × WorkingDay.Hours × Hands`.

If output covers use, do nothing. Otherwise the shortfall is `use − output`, and the AI takes on hands
in the branches that produce the commodity, province by province in the country's order, until the
shortfall is covered or nothing more can be done. For each such branch:

```
per hand   = OutputPerHour.Amount × WorkingDay.Hours
wanted     = ceiling(remaining shortfall / per hand)
available  = province.Idle(branch.Workforce)
equippable = the most hands the store's instruments allow
             (unlimited when the branch uses no instruments; otherwise the smallest, over its
              instruments, of floor(store amount / instrument amount per current hand))
hire       = min(wanted, available, equippable)
```

If `hire > 0`, call `country.Employ(province, commodity, hire)`.

A commodity the country has no branch for is skipped. Taking on hands in a branch that uses material
raises the use of that material; that is corrected the following month, not in the same pass.

### Rule 2 — end the wars it is winning

For each open war the country is in where it is `Prevailing`:

- if `|Momentum|` is 100: propose `Peace.Annexation()`;
- otherwise, if `ProvincesWithinReach` is at least 2 and the loser governs more than one province:
  propose `Peace.Ceding(...)` of the loser's first `n` provinces in the loser's order, where
  `n = min(ProvincesWithinReach, loser's provinces − 1)`;
- otherwise wait.

The AI never proposes a white peace and never ends a war it is losing.

## Console

No new command. AI actions are not reported as occurrences; their effects show up in `country <n>`
(plan 07). A peace an AI makes is reported through `PeaceWasMade` (plan 08), like any other.

## Out of scope

Declaring war, legislating, re-equipping, opening or closing branches, laying off, any notion of
strategy, difficulty levels, randomness.

## Done when

Build and tests are clean, including the five-year test.
